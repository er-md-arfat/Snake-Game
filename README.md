*SNAKE GAME*
*Game Instructions:*
1. Press ARROW keys to control the snake head.
2. Colliding with goals/food will lengthen the snake.
3. Colliding with walls or the tail of your snake will end the game.

*LEVELS :*
1. Level 0: Score 0 to Score 50 (Speed Slow)
2. Level 1: Score 50 to Score 100 (Speed Medium)
3. Level 2: Score 100 to Score 150 (Speed Fast)
4. Level 3: Score 150 and Above (Speed Most Faster)
